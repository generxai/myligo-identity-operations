# MyLigo Identity Operations

A working concept for discussion, prepared by GenerXAI. This enterprise product demo covers hospital onboarding, an identity exception queue, agent-assisted review, human approval, patient consent, immutable audit provenance, source-system controls, identity policies, and operational measurement.

## Primary demo path

1. Start in **Exception queue**.
2. Open **IR-2841 — Marisol Vega**.
3. Review the agent explanation and field-level source evidence.
4. Inspect **Consent** and **History**.
5. Select **Approve merge**, attest that the evidence was reviewed, and approve reconciliation.
6. Open **Consent & audit** to see the timestamped human approval.
7. Open **Operations** to review volume, backlog, automation, staffing load, and implementation speed.

## Other complete surfaces

- Hospital onboarding with system readiness, mapping, security tasks, blockers, owners, and go-live state
- Source systems with feed health, latency, mapping coverage, and event throughput
- Identity policies with automation thresholds and mandatory human-review rules

## Data and safety

All patient names, hospital names, identifiers, events, metrics, and organizations in this artifact are wholly synthetic. They are constructed to feel operationally credible but do not describe real people, customers, deployments, or outcomes.

This is a non-production concept. It does not make clinical decisions, establish regulatory compliance, or imply approval by or an existing relationship with MyLigo. MyLigo branding is presented only as the proposed product name supplied for the concept. GenerXAI is credited as the preparer.
